# Boldleonidas Comic Meme Style Guide

This document defines the visual and content styling rules extracted from the analysis of 701 meme images from the `@boldleonidas` account. Use this guide to programmatically or conceptually construct new comic memes in the signature style.

---

## 🎨 1. Visual & Aesthetic Identity

### 1.1 Color Palette & Backgrounds
*   **Background Style**: Minimalistic. Mostly flat, solid colors or subtle gradients to keep the focus entirely on characters.
*   **Common Colors**:
    *   *Neutral Grays/Light Grays*: Used in over 50% of the memes to create a clean, modern look.
    *   *Soft Pastel Gradients*: Cloudy skies, sunrise/sunset gradients for outdoor settings.
    *   *Solid Black/Dark Navy*: Reserved for night scenes (e.g., character working under a desk lamp) or high-contrast dramatic panels.
    *   *Green and Red*: Symbolizing market candles (Bitcoin/Crypto charts). Green candles are tall, upward, and bring joy; red candles are downward and bring despair.
*   **Borders**: A clean, thin white border often wraps around the panels or the entire image to separate it from the social platform feed.

### 1.2 Layout & Panel Composition
*   **Single-Panel Layout (81.0% of memes)**:
    *   Best for quick reactions, single-sentence observations, or chart-based metaphors.
    *   *Structure*: A main character positioned center/left with a single speech bubble, or reacting to a large chart/element on the opposite side.
*   **Two-Panel Layout (15.7% of memes)**:
    *   Best for dialogues, negotiations, and setup/punchline logic.
    *   *Structure*: Typically divided horizontally (two equal panels side-by-side or stacked vertically). Characters maintain consistent positioning across both panels (e.g., Wizard on the left, Frog on the right).
*   **Multi-Panel (3+ panels, ~3.3% of memes)**:
    *   Used for progressive storytelling or comic strips (e.g., knocking on a door, opening it, presenting the punchline).

## 🎭 2. Character Encyclopedia

Characters are hand-drawn, expressive, and cartoonish. They occupy **30% to 40%** of their respective panels.

| Character | Visual Description | Archetype / Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wizard / Mage** (37.9%) | Purple robe with yellow stars/moon patterns, pointed wizard hat, long white beard, and expressive eyebrows. | The sage, advisor, mentor, or representative of "smart money/tech devs". Often giving warnings or advice. |
| **Green Frog (Pepe-like)** (11.1%) | Green skin, large expressive eyes, occasionally wearing simple shirts or hats. | The retail investor, skeptic, or worried speculator. Represents the "average crypto user". |
| **Orange Bitcoin Coin** (5.1%) | A round, orange coin representing the Bitcoin symbol with cartoonish arms, legs, and face. | Represents Bitcoin itself, market dominance, or the crypto market force. |
| **Purple 'PRO' Hat Character** (4.7%) | Purple body/face, wearing a black cap with "PRO" written in bold white letters. | The professional developer, experienced trader, or confident builder. |
| **Pig** (2.9%) | Pink, round pig with wide eyes and simple features. | Often the partner in dialogue memes, acting as the naive counterpoint or victim of a bad trade. |
| **Blue Drop Character (Sui)** (2.4%) | Water drop-shaped blue character with eyes and hands. | Used specifically in project collaboration memes, usually standing side-by-side with the Wizard. |

## 💬 3. Text, Fonts & Speech Bubbles

### 3.1 Speech Bubble Design
*   **Appearance**: Perfect white background, outlined in a medium-weight black border.
*   **Bubble Tails**: Straight or slightly curved, pointing directly to the mouth or head of the speaking character.
*   **Placement**: Positioned in the upper region of the panel (top-left or top-right) above the speaker's head to prevent overlapping the characters.

### 3.2 Font & Typography Style
*   **Style**: Hand-drawn, slightly irregular, bold sans-serif comic font (similar to Comic Sans or hand-written marker pen).
*   **Casing**: **ALL CAPS** is standard for dialogue. Sentence case is occasionally used for labels/captions.
*   **Text Color**: Black text on white bubbles. For charts or badges, colors match the context (e.g., light green for "BUY", red for "SELL" or "OPINION").

---

## 🧠 4. Content Structure & Tone

*   **Reaction/Emotion (61.9%)**: Characters exhibiting extreme expressions of joy (upon seeing a green candle), shock/despair (seeing a red candle), or smug confidence.
*   **Dialogue Comic (18.8%)**: A humorous negotiation or conversation. A typical pattern:
    *   *Speaker A (Left)*: Asks a question or makes an offer (e.g., "Best I can do is $36,000").
    *   *Speaker B (Right)*: Reacts with shock or a witty counter-statement.
*   **Juxtaposition/Comparison (18.7%)**: Contrasting two different states (e.g., "Old Algorithm vs New Algorithm", "Stocks vs Crypto", "Before vs After Airdrop").
