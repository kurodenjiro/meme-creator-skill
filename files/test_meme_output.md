### Concept Analysis

The idea of this meme is to humorously capture the reaction of a Green Frog, representing the average crypto enthusiast, to the shocking and unexpected news that Trump has declared crypto the official reserve asset of the US. The Green Frog’s expression shifts from disbelief to excitement, highlighting the absurdity and surprise of the situation.

### Meme Blueprint

**Primary Character:** Green Frog

**Panel 2:**
